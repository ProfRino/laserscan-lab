Training guide & limitations

Start with the four animation steps, then try the six lessons in order.

Occlusion exercise: scan a wall and table, enable Cloud only and gap markers, then add a second station on the other side. Compare visibility and overlap before and after.

Check your understanding: Why does doubling range double spacing? Why can finer resolution not see behind a wall? Why do three collinear targets make registration unstable?

Drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. In Top view drag to pan. Drag tripods or objects to move them. Touch: one finger rotates; two fingers pan and zoom.

Coverage samples the outer room shell on a 0.5 m grid. It estimates geometric visibility, not actual point density, furniture coverage, or registration quality. Noise and grazing-angle dropout are random. Stations are successive positions of one scanner and do not block each other. The registration lab uses six fixed targets to connect both rooms through the corridor.

The multi-room lesson shows how fixed reference balls connect sequential scans. It keeps the simulated coordinates aligned to illustrate the survey workflow; it does not fit targets or run a registration solver.

Educational model: single-return laser, ideal reflectivity, no multipath or mixed pixels. Field time assumes ≈50 k pts/s plus 3 min setup per station.
